We built wheez because delivery got expensive.

Order through a delivery app and the same meal often arrives smaller, at a higher price than you would pay walking into the restaurant. Groceries and essentials carry the same quiet markup, all before service and delivery fees are added. Wheez closes that gap, so you keep the apps and stores you love and pay what things should actually cost.

Our mission

Make everyday delivery cheaper for everyone.
